FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of SAP courses?

Course duration varies by module. Most programs are 45–90 days with weekday and weekend batch options to suit working professionals and fresh graduates.

Do I need prior IT experience?

No prior IT experience is needed. Courses are designed for beginners. Basic computer knowledge and logical thinking are sufficient — trainers guide you from fundamentals to advanced levels.

How does the 100% Placement Guarantee work?

Our placement team maintains relationships with top SAP employers, provides resume building, mock interviews, and direct referrals — supporting you until you receive a job offer.

Is online training available?

Yes! We offer both in-person classroom training at our Belgaum center and online live sessions. Contact us at +91 98867 78997 for current batch schedules.

Will I receive a certificate after completion?

Yes. On successfully completing the course and project, you will receive an Innovic Solutions certificate. We also guide you through official SAP certification exam preparation.
